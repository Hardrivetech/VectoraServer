# Code of Conduct
## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and safe community, we as contributors and maintainers of Vectora Server pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of experience level, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

Vectora Server is a minimal custom Minecraft Java Edition server written in C. We welcome people who are experimenting, learning, and building with us — but we will not tolerate harassment or abusive behavior.
Expected Behavior

    Be kind, respectful, and patient.
    Assume good faith in others’ intentions.
    Use welcoming and inclusive language.
    Give constructive feedback and criticism; focus on the work, not the person.
    Respect privacy and do not share others’ personal information.
    When you disagree, do so respectfully and provide reasoning.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include, but are not limited to:

    Harassment, intimidation, or stalking.
    Derogatory comments, slurs, or demeaning language.
    Personal attacks, including insults about a person’s competence, background, or identity.
    Publishing others’ private information without consent.
    Threats of violence or any violent language.
    Sustained disruption of project spaces (issue trackers, pull requests, chat channels).
    Sexual language or imagery in inappropriate contexts.
    Discrimination or exclusionary behavior.

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and activities, including:

    Issue trackers, pull requests, and code discussions on the repository.
    Official and unofficial chat channels, forums, mailing lists, and social media where project-related conversation occurs.
    Events sponsored by the project (virtual or in-person).
    Behavior by anyone representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers at: Vectora-Contact@protonmail.com. Please include as much information as you can (dates/times, links, screenshots, and a description of what happened). Reports will be treated seriously and handled with as much confidentiality as possible.

If you prefer not to use email, you may open a new issue titled "CODE OF CONDUCT REPORT" and include the relevant details; please note that public issues are less private.

## Enforcement

All reports will be reviewed and investigated by the maintainers or an appointed conduct team. Possible responses include:

    A private warning to the individual involved.
    Temporary or permanent removal of access to project resources (issue tracker, chat, repository).
    Public or private statements to the community if appropriate.
    Reporting to platform hosts if platform rules were violated.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith will not be tolerated.

## Moderation Process

    Reports will be handled by the maintainers or an appointed conduct team.
    The reporting party should provide as much information as possible: timestamps, links, screenshots, and a description of the incident.
    The maintainers will acknowledge receipt within a reasonable timeframe and keep the reporter updated.
    The maintainers may confidentially consult others if needed.
    The goal is to resolve issues fairly while protecting the safety and dignity of all participants.
